STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: COMMERCIAL LOAN APPLICATION REVIEW
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-FINANCE-010
VERSION: 1.1
EFFECTIVE DATE: DECEMBER 2024

1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES
Standardizes operations, compliance checkpoints, and handoffs across organizational tiers for commercial loan application review.

2. PARTICIPATING ACTORS
- Borrower: Initiates trigger event and provides source context.
- Loan Officer: Reviews inputs against regulatory and operational standards.
- Credit Underwriter: Performs primary execution, mitigation, or analytical assessment.
- Credit Committee: Validates resolution sign-off and records audit trails.

3. OPERATIONAL PHASES

[Chunk 0: Intake & Validation]
The process starts when Borrower prepares and submits the standardized package into the operations management system. Loan Officer verifies submission completeness and logs the transaction.

[Chunk 1: Assessment and Standards Compliance]
Loan Officer evaluates the request against current organizational risk guidelines, service level commitments, and standard criteria.

[Chunk 2: Governance Decision Gate]
Loan Officer renders an approval determination:
- If adjustments are required or parameters fail baseline criteria, the request returns to Borrower for revisions.
- If criteria are met, the request advances to Credit Underwriter for technical processing.

[Chunk 3: Execution and Verification]
Credit Underwriter executes the planned operations, tests operational integrity, and compiles supporting artifacts into the master tracking file.

[Chunk 4: Final Sign-off & Audit Closure]
Credit Committee reviews execution outcomes, grants final authorization, updates enterprise registers, and archives the case documentation.
