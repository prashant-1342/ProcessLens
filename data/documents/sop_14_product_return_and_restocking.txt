STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: WAREHOUSE PRODUCT RETURN & RESTOCKING
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-LOGISTICS-014
VERSION: 1.5
EFFECTIVE DATE: DECEMBER 2024

1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES
Standardizes operations, compliance checkpoints, and handoffs across organizational tiers for warehouse product return & restocking.

2. PARTICIPATING ACTORS
- Customer: Initiates trigger event and provides source context.
- Receiving Clerk: Reviews inputs against regulatory and operational standards.
- QA Inspector: Performs primary execution, mitigation, or analytical assessment.
- Inventory Manager: Validates resolution sign-off and records audit trails.

3. OPERATIONAL PHASES

[Chunk 0: Intake & Validation]
The process starts when Customer prepares and submits the standardized package into the operations management system. Receiving Clerk verifies submission completeness and logs the transaction.

[Chunk 1: Assessment and Standards Compliance]
Receiving Clerk evaluates the request against current organizational risk guidelines, service level commitments, and standard criteria.

[Chunk 2: Governance Decision Gate]
Receiving Clerk renders an approval determination:
- If adjustments are required or parameters fail baseline criteria, the request returns to Customer for revisions.
- If criteria are met, the request advances to QA Inspector for technical processing.

[Chunk 3: Execution and Verification]
QA Inspector executes the planned operations, tests operational integrity, and compiles supporting artifacts into the master tracking file.

[Chunk 4: Final Sign-off & Audit Closure]
Inventory Manager reviews execution outcomes, grants final authorization, updates enterprise registers, and archives the case documentation.
