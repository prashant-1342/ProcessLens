STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: MONTHLY CORPORATE PAYROLL EXECUTION
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-HR-020
VERSION: 1.1
EFFECTIVE DATE: DECEMBER 2024

1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES
Standardizes operations, compliance checkpoints, and handoffs across organizational tiers for monthly corporate payroll execution.

2. PARTICIPATING ACTORS
- Payroll Officer: Initiates trigger event and provides source context.
- HR Director: Reviews inputs against regulatory and operational standards.
- Treasury Lead: Performs primary execution, mitigation, or analytical assessment.
- Internal Auditor: Validates resolution sign-off and records audit trails.

3. OPERATIONAL PHASES

[Chunk 0: Intake & Validation]
The process starts when Payroll Officer prepares and submits the standardized package into the operations management system. HR Director verifies submission completeness and logs the transaction.

[Chunk 1: Assessment and Standards Compliance]
HR Director evaluates the request against current organizational risk guidelines, service level commitments, and standard criteria.

[Chunk 2: Governance Decision Gate]
HR Director renders an approval determination:
- If adjustments are required or parameters fail baseline criteria, the request returns to Payroll Officer for revisions.
- If criteria are met, the request advances to Treasury Lead for technical processing.

[Chunk 3: Execution and Verification]
Treasury Lead executes the planned operations, tests operational integrity, and compiles supporting artifacts into the master tracking file.

[Chunk 4: Final Sign-off & Audit Closure]
Internal Auditor reviews execution outcomes, grants final authorization, updates enterprise registers, and archives the case documentation.
