STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE AUTOMATED ALERT REMEDIATION
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-CLOUD-019
VERSION: 1.5
EFFECTIVE DATE: DECEMBER 2024

1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES
Standardizes operations, compliance checkpoints, and handoffs across organizational tiers for cloud infrastructure automated alert remediation.

2. PARTICIPATING ACTORS
- Cloud Monitoring Engine: Initiates trigger event and provides source context.
- On-Call Engineer: Reviews inputs against regulatory and operational standards.
- Platform Lead: Performs primary execution, mitigation, or analytical assessment.
- Security Auditor: Validates resolution sign-off and records audit trails.

3. OPERATIONAL PHASES

[Chunk 0: Intake & Validation]
The process starts when Cloud Monitoring Engine prepares and submits the standardized package into the operations management system. On-Call Engineer verifies submission completeness and logs the transaction.

[Chunk 1: Assessment and Standards Compliance]
On-Call Engineer evaluates the request against current organizational risk guidelines, service level commitments, and standard criteria.

[Chunk 2: Governance Decision Gate]
On-Call Engineer renders an approval determination:
- If adjustments are required or parameters fail baseline criteria, the request returns to Cloud Monitoring Engine for revisions.
- If criteria are met, the request advances to Platform Lead for technical processing.

[Chunk 3: Execution and Verification]
Platform Lead executes the planned operations, tests operational integrity, and compiles supporting artifacts into the master tracking file.

[Chunk 4: Final Sign-off & Audit Closure]
Security Auditor reviews execution outcomes, grants final authorization, updates enterprise registers, and archives the case documentation.
