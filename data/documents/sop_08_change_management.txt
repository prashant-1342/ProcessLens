STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: IT INFRASTRUCTURE CHANGE MANAGEMENT
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-OPERATIONS-008
VERSION: 1.4
EFFECTIVE DATE: DECEMBER 2024

1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES
Standardizes operations, compliance checkpoints, and handoffs across organizational tiers for it infrastructure change management.

2. PARTICIPATING ACTORS
- Change Requester: Initiates trigger event and provides source context.
- CAB Chair: Reviews inputs against regulatory and operational standards.
- Release Engineer: Performs primary execution, mitigation, or analytical assessment.
- Site Reliability Engineer: Validates resolution sign-off and records audit trails.

3. OPERATIONAL PHASES

[Chunk 0: Intake & Validation]
The process starts when Change Requester prepares and submits the standardized package into the operations management system. CAB Chair verifies submission completeness and logs the transaction.

[Chunk 1: Assessment and Standards Compliance]
CAB Chair evaluates the request against current organizational risk guidelines, service level commitments, and standard criteria.

[Chunk 2: Governance Decision Gate]
CAB Chair renders an approval determination:
- If adjustments are required or parameters fail baseline criteria, the request returns to Change Requester for revisions.
- If criteria are met, the request advances to Release Engineer for technical processing.

[Chunk 3: Execution and Verification]
Release Engineer executes the planned operations, tests operational integrity, and compiles supporting artifacts into the master tracking file.

[Chunk 4: Final Sign-off & Audit Closure]
Site Reliability Engineer reviews execution outcomes, grants final authorization, updates enterprise registers, and archives the case documentation.
