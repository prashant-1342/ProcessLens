STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: VENDOR PROCUREMENT & CONTRACT AWARD
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-PROCUREMENT-006
VERSION: 1.2
EFFECTIVE DATE: DECEMBER 2024

1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES
Standardizes operations, compliance checkpoints, and handoffs across organizational tiers for vendor procurement & contract award.

2. PARTICIPATING ACTORS
- Procurement Specialist: Initiates trigger event and provides source context.
- Legal Counsel: Reviews inputs against regulatory and operational standards.
- Finance Controller: Performs primary execution, mitigation, or analytical assessment.
- Supplier: Validates resolution sign-off and records audit trails.

3. OPERATIONAL PHASES

[Chunk 0: Intake & Validation]
The process starts when Procurement Specialist prepares and submits the standardized package into the operations management system. Legal Counsel verifies submission completeness and logs the transaction.

[Chunk 1: Assessment and Standards Compliance]
Legal Counsel evaluates the request against current organizational risk guidelines, service level commitments, and standard criteria.

[Chunk 2: Governance Decision Gate]
Legal Counsel renders an approval determination:
- If adjustments are required or parameters fail baseline criteria, the request returns to Procurement Specialist for revisions.
- If criteria are met, the request advances to Finance Controller for technical processing.

[Chunk 3: Execution and Verification]
Finance Controller executes the planned operations, tests operational integrity, and compiles supporting artifacts into the master tracking file.

[Chunk 4: Final Sign-off & Audit Closure]
Supplier reviews execution outcomes, grants final authorization, updates enterprise registers, and archives the case documentation.
