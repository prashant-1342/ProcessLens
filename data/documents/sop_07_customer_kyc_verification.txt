STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: CUSTOMER KNOW-YOUR-CUSTOMER VERIFICATION
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-COMPLIANCE-007
VERSION: 1.3
EFFECTIVE DATE: DECEMBER 2024

1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES
Standardizes operations, compliance checkpoints, and handoffs across organizational tiers for customer know-your-customer verification.

2. PARTICIPATING ACTORS
- Customer: Initiates trigger event and provides source context.
- Compliance Officer: Reviews inputs against regulatory and operational standards.
- Risk Analyst: Performs primary execution, mitigation, or analytical assessment.
- Senior Auditor: Validates resolution sign-off and records audit trails.

3. OPERATIONAL PHASES

[Chunk 0: Intake & Validation]
The process starts when Customer prepares and submits the standardized package into the operations management system. Compliance Officer verifies submission completeness and logs the transaction.

[Chunk 1: Assessment and Standards Compliance]
Compliance Officer evaluates the request against current organizational risk guidelines, service level commitments, and standard criteria.

[Chunk 2: Governance Decision Gate]
Compliance Officer renders an approval determination:
- If adjustments are required or parameters fail baseline criteria, the request returns to Customer for revisions.
- If criteria are met, the request advances to Risk Analyst for technical processing.

[Chunk 3: Execution and Verification]
Risk Analyst executes the planned operations, tests operational integrity, and compiles supporting artifacts into the master tracking file.

[Chunk 4: Final Sign-off & Audit Closure]
Senior Auditor reviews execution outcomes, grants final authorization, updates enterprise registers, and archives the case documentation.
